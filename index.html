About

I started with science — studying how biological systems work at the molecular level. Then I became curious about what happens around science: how discoveries become products, how products find markets, how markets shape behaviour.

That curiosity led me toward commerce, then strategy. I started leading teams — first small ones, then a 61-person society. I learned to secure sponsorships, plan events, manage budgets, build content systems and ship campaigns.

Somewhere along the way, I realised the pattern: I keep moving between understanding something and making it work. Between the idea and the execution. Between the insight and the organisation it requires.

Now I want to understand how businesses actually operate — how strategy becomes structure, how products become companies, how ideas become industries.
